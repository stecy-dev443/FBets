A considered palette for play

Midnight
Gold.

A little restraint goes a long way. Midnight Gold gives the sportsbook and casino a shared visual language: deep, quiet surfaces; warm, readable type; and gold used only when something deserves attention.

The aim is confidence, not noise. The interface should feel composed before a single market is opened.

02 / Tonality

Depth, not decoration

The ramps move from pale tints to grounded darks. They are intended to make state and hierarchy consistent across every screen, not to fill every available space with colour.

04 / Interface

Small moments matter

The gold button is the signature. Odds remain quiet until selected; movement is shown with direction as well as colour; suspended prices look unavailable, not broken.

05 / Legibility

Contrast is part of the craft

The following pairs are the combinations intended for real interface text. Ratios are calculated using the WCAG relative-luminance method; normal text needs at least 4.5:1.

The gold rule. Gold on midnight ink reads at 10.50:1. On a gold button, use dark ink (#241A0E), not white. Dark ink stays above AA even against the deepest gold gradient stop.

Win and loss never rely on colour alone: keep the arrow and the word beside the value. The same principle applies to LIVE, BOOST and SUSPENDED.
